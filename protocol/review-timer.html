AII-600 Part 1 — Review timer

Protocol: 1) Preview the 12 frames without counting and choose clarity. 2) Press Start timer. Count each frame under counting-rule.md, enter counts and notes. 3) Press Finish. Use Pause (interruption) when attention leaves the task; paused time is logged as interruption seconds, not review time. If you lose your place, use Restart (lost place). Progress is saved in this browser (localStorage) — export the CSV when done.

Step 1 — Rate clarity (not timed)

Look at the typical frame difficulty. Do not enumerate cars yet.

Clarity definitions

clear: parked passenger cars separate from background at a glance; distinct edges; usable light; second looks rare.

moderately unclear: needs extra attention (shadow/glare, moderate occlusion, small/distant cars, mild blur, packed cars) but most objects classifiable without guessing.

very unclear: a substantial share of candidates need guesswork or fail the rule (severe dark, glare, heavy occlusion, extreme distance, poor focus).
